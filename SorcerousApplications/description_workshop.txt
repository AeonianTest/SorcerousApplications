[h1]Sorcerous Applications[/h1]

A small content mod focused on adding more early game magical tools

[h2]Content[/h2]

[b]Soul Fragment[/b]
Crafting material dropped by Skeletons and Ghosts.

[b]Foci[/b]
A soul-bound focus for close-range magical thrust attacks.

[b]Flame Staff[/b]
Channel to charge, then release a short-range burst of fire.

[b]Force Staff[/b]
Channel to charge, then launch a heavy orb with strong knockback. Pierces enemies and tiles.

[h2]Status[/h2]

Early developmen, pretty much everything subject to change via how I feel about things
